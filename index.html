    §01 · A grammar machine for authorised research

    Enigmista.

    Models the structure of an authorised password corpus and generates new, previously-unseen candidates that fit its distribution — for policy audits, honeyword decoys, synthetic data, and research.

    enigmista (it.) — one who crafts and solves enigmas

    §02 · Structure

    It reads the shape of a corpus, never the secrets of a person.

    Give it a seed corpus you're authorised to analyse — a population of passwords — and it learns the grammar underneath: the templates people reach for, the slots they fill.

    Neural · the language model

    Induces the grammar from the seed — names the themes, sorts the slots, and extends the dictionaries with plausible members it was never shown.

    Symbolic · sampler & scorer

    A deterministic sampler draws candidates in probability order; a scorer checks them against real passwords held out from training. The model proposes; the symbolic layer owns the probability.

    §03 · Uses

    Three uses. All defensive.

    ⟨audit⟩ · Password-policy audits

    Test a policy you're authorised to audit against the realistic structure of the passwords it governs.

    ⟨decoy⟩ · Honeywords & decoys

    Decoys that fit the corpus's distribution. The more plausible the decoy, the more reliably a login with it signals a breach.

    ⟨synth⟩ · Synthetic data & research

    Password sets with the statistical shape of the real thing — for testing systems and studying how people choose.

    §04 · Objective

    Predict the passwords you haven't seen.

    A model that memorises its seed is worthless — it only regenerates what it was given. Enigmista is judged on a frozen held-out split it never trained on.

    The kernel, in geometry

    Below the drum lies what it draws from: the toy grammar as a disc of probability. The inner ring splits the circle by template; each ring outward splits its slice by the next slot's terms. A slice's angle is the chance a draw lands in it; the towers on the rim are whole passwords.

    Temperature · τ

    A sample is a dart thrown at a uniform angle and read outward, ring by ring. τ reshapes the disc: low τ piles the mass onto a few tall modes; high τ flattens the skyline and widens the tails, where the held-out passwords wait.

    §05 · Method

    A loop that only believes its scorer.

    induce → sample → score → analyse misses → refine, until coverage plateaus or hits a target.

    01 · Corpus & protocol

    Load the authorised seed, deduplicate, freeze an 80/20 train / held-out split — once, never re-split. Markov and PCFG baselines set the number to beat.

    02 · Induce

    A language model builds the grammar from the training split; on a frontier model, an enrichment pass adds plausible unseen terms.

    03 · Sample

    Draw a budget of candidates at temperature τ, ordered by probability — the guess order.

    04 · Score

    The bundled scorer measures coverage of the held-out split. Never self-assessed: no improvement is claimed without its numbers.

    05 · Analyse misses & refine

    Read the held-out passwords it missed, hypothesise the missing structure, refine the grammar, go round again.

    06 · Stop

    When coverage beats the best baseline and gains plateau — or the iteration cap is reached.

    §06 · Sandbox

    Illustrative · toy data

    A grammar from sixteen fake passwords.

    A tiny inducer, run in your browser on a corpus we invented. Nowhere to type a password, nothing sent anywhere — it illustrates induce → sample; it is not the engine.

    01 · Segment

    Each password splits into slots by character class: word, year, digit, symbol.

    02 · Count templates

    Passwords with the same slot pattern stack together. The stacks are the template probabilities.

    03 · Fill slots

    Each slot keeps the terms the seed used, weighted by how often. From the seed alone, of the held-out passwords are reachable.

    04 · Enrich (scripted here)

    Plausible terms the seed never showed are added — in the real loop a frontier model proposes them. Now are reachable — reachable, not yet covered.

    05 · Sample

    The Rotor draws from the induced grammar — every candidate on the right is one spin. Move τ and sample again.

            §07 · Scope

            The corpus boundary is the whole line.

            The same machinery is defensive on a corpus and an attack on a person. Enigmista stays on one side of that line by design.

            In scope

            Policy audits you're authorised to run. Honeywords and decoys. Synthetic data and research — always from a seed corpus you're authorised to use: a population, never one person.

            Out of scope — refused

            A person's name, birthday or pet used to guess their passwords. Candidates aimed at someone's accounts. One person's passwords passed off as a corpus.

            When asked to cross it

            Enigmista names the boundary, offers the legitimate version — model a corpus you're authorised to use — and stops. Its candidate lists and grammars stay in a local ./out/.

            This site

            Keeps the same rule: a bundled toy list, nowhere to type a password, no analytics, no trackers.

            §08 · Install

            Run the real loop.

            Enigmista ships as a skill for runtimes with a disk and script execution: Claude Code, Gemini CLI, or a local model on Hermes. It hands back at every checkpoint.

            01 · Get & install

            unzip enigmista-skill.zip -d ~/.claude/skills/

            Download the skill Latest GitHub release · Gemini CLI and Hermes steps are in the release notes.

            02 · Smoke-test the scorer

            python3 scripts/eval_coverage.py \
  --heldout assets/sample/normal/heldout.txt \
  --candidates assets/sample/normal/candidates.jsonl \
  --train assets/sample/normal/train.txt \
  --baseline assets/sample/normal/baseline_coverage.json \
  --out /tmp/out.json --cutoffs 5 10 100 --primary 100
SUMMARY: cov@100=75.0% (hit 3/4 held-out) Δbaseline=+25.0pts novelty=57.14% cands=14

            03 · Bring an authorised corpus

            Put your seed in data/seed.txt and start the loop — baselines come back first. Prefer to talk it through? The Gem on Gemini writes the grammar and commands, but never reports coverage as measured: enigmista-gem.zip.
